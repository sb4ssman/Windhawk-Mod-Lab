# Privacy Anchor candidate: Recall / OneDrive

Local candidate only; header remains 2.1. Install the candidate through Windhawk's
editor yourself, preserving your current source/settings as needed.

1. Enable Content > Recall and Content > OneDrive (both default off).
2. Try `location | mic | camera | copilot | recall | onedrive`, then
   `location, mic, camera, copilot, recall, onedrive`, and `auto`.
3. Hover the new icons. They must explicitly describe process/policy evidence
   and must not claim verified capture/sync. No process means unknown, not off.
4. Compare OneDrive running/stopped and Recall's native state where available.
   The new bright icon reports named-process presence; use native icons for
   saving, filtering, syncing, paused, and error states. Native icons stay intact.
5. Check inherited colors, opacity, glow and a policy slash where an existing
   disabling policy applies. Do not change policy just for this test.
6. Click the new slots: Recall opens privacy settings; OneDrive opens installed
   apps. Switch taskbar edges and check layout, then disable/re-enable the mod.
7. Confirm existing mic/location/camera/Copilot behavior has not regressed.

Cloud evidence reconciles every minute while enabled, at load/settings changes,
and during the five-minute general reconciliation. This is not instant capture
or sync-state detection. Fresh screenshot and explicit approval are needed before
any publication. No installed source or settings were changed by the agent.
