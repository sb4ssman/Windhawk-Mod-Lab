Thanks. The blocking item was real: the new battery-slot flags and the old "a slot appeared" inference disagreed, and the result would have been a rebuild on every layout pass.

**1. Child-count inference.** Fixed as you suggested. `ApplyLayout` records the items host's child count it actually saw, `ResetElementRefs` clears it to -1, and the monitor compares the live count against it. That keeps the original case (apply ran before the children were realized) and also catches a child being removed, which nothing noticed before. One guard beyond your sketch: the comparison is skipped while the recorded count is -1. That covers an apply that bailed out before counting (`IsItemsHost()` false), where comparing against -1 would itself have rebuilt on every pass.

**Optional items, all taken:**
- **Stale `omni_retry` comment:** it now says that `Start()` may run on Windhawk's thread and the taskbar thread at once, and that the exchange handles it.
- **Dead component code:** the mod now uses the plain arrangement component rather than the axis-relative variant, so `AlongAxis`, `axisRelative`, `thickness`/`cross`, and the axis-relative branches in `MeasureNode`, `ConcreteSize` and `Compute` are no longer in the file. `ComputeOmniNode` calls a new `ComputeTree` (measure + arrange of an already-parsed tree, sharing one cache) instead of the uncached `Measure`/`Arrange`, which were removed from the component. The assembler also prunes a component's optional entry points by reference now, so `TokenIndexWithPrefix`, `Lease::RestoreObject`/`Abandon`/`Count`/`Empty`, `Metrics::alongDip` and `forceFirstAttempt` are gone too (`forceFirstAttempt` is gone from the component itself). The two comments pointing at `_templates/` files are rewritten.
- **`setting.get() ? setting.get() : L""`:** now just `setting.get()`.
- **Includes:** `<cwchar>` and `<cwctype>` are included explicitly.
- **`no_destroy` on `g_retryLoop`:** removed, and marked `exit-time-safe: heap-only` like the other heap-only globals.

**Functionality notes:**
- **Geometry re-apply bypassing the vertical stand-down:** taken, in the cheaper form you suggested. When the monitor sees the taskbar turn vertical, it logs, hands everything back (`CleanupAndResetCurrentElements` under `ApplyingScope`), revokes itself, and clears `g_applied`. The next `TrayUI::StartTaskbar` re-evaluates.
- **Positional network/volume slots:** left as the documented limitation.

The rebuild hook, which runs on the taskbar thread, also no longer restarts the retry loop and waits for the old run. It wakes the live run through the loop's new `StartOrWake()`.
